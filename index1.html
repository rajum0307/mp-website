About me

Born and raised in the beautiful city of Visakhapatnam, I've witnessed firsthand the challenges and opportunities that shape our community. My journey into public service began during my college years when I started volunteering for social causes.

After completing my engineering degree, I worked in the private sector for several years. However, the calling to serve my people remained strong. In 2019, I took the leap into politics, driven by a passion for sustainable development and youth empowerment.

Today, I'm honored to represent Visakhapatnam in Parliament, working tirelessly to bring development, create opportunities, and ensure that every voice in our constituency is heard and respected.
