Community Guidelines

✅ Do's

  • • Be respectful and courteous in all communications
  • • Share constructive feedback and suggestions
  • • Report issues and concerns promptly
  • • Participate actively in community discussions
  • • Verify information before sharing
  • • Support fellow community members

❌ Don'ts

  • • Share false or misleading information
  • • Use offensive or inappropriate language
  • • Promote personal business interests
  • • Share personal information of others
  • • Spam or send irrelevant content
  • • Engage in political arguments or debates
